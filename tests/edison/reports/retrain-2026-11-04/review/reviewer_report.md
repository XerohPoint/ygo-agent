**Findings and their classification.**

1. **REACHABLE ? the opponent is limited and receives fewer public inputs. High confidence, read and run.** `ygoai/edison_policy.py:85` initializes the activation whitelist; `:110` explicitly refuses Solemn Judgment/Royal Oppression/Limiter Removal; `:112` scores unsafe activations below cancellation. `actor.py:154`, `:256` uses the original Heuristic, not ContextHeuristic, passing only cards/global/actions. The net also receives history and decision/selection context (`actor.py:211`). In the 1,008 new duels the heuristic chose zero of 857 offered Substitoad activations, 339 Kalut chain activations, 259 Honest chain activations, and 1,232 Solemn Judgment activations. Offers can repeat within a game; these are not distinct games or a claim that every declined offer was tactically correct to accept. This is the declared frozen opponent, not a substituted weaker one. It limits the meaning of the result to outperforming that opponent; its contribution to the study's margin is not causally quantified.

2. **TEST ? 50% aggregate mirror controls cannot detect bias. High confidence, run.** `evaluate.py:50` runs both challenger seats with identical heuristic/heuristic policies; `final_test.py:108` reports the aggregate. I checked the final raw cells: each paired seat has identical complete physical duel records, 1,536 fixture starts and 2,688 gauntlet starts duplicated. Each decisive duel necessarily contributes one challenger win and one challenger loss. Even an engine awarding every duel to physical seat 0 would produce 50%. The controls are useful determinism observations, but their aggregate 50% is not a fairness or strength calibration. No scoring inflation of network games follows from this alone.

3. **TEST ? replay traces omit network history. High confidence, source and executed synthetic mutation of a real observation.** `actor.py:266` hashes cards, selection, global, offered actions, decision and chosen action, but never `h_actions_`; `evaluate.py:124` compares resulting duel records. On a real turn-3 target observation at seed 84002000 I changed a valid history row's effect column from 0 to 1. The observation/history changed, while the trace input digest was identical (`reviewer_supplement.py`). A history-only discrepancy can therefore pass the replay check when chosen actions/results remain unchanged. I found no actual historical history divergence. My metamorphic tests compare history directly.

4. **REACHABLE ? pooled passing conditions do not mean every combination wins. High confidence, run.** `final/protocol.md:22` and `final_test.py:92` require marginal deck and seat pools. Actual optimizer 1703 with Blackwing in seat 1 wins 254/512=49.61%; pooled Blackwing seat 1 is 800/1536=52.08%. Across optimizers and both deck orders, network Blackwing versus heuristic Machina Gadget wins 424/1536=27.60%. This does not violate F1. It rules out claiming consistent superiority in every matchup or every optimizer/deck/seat intersection.

5. **REACHABLE ? test decks and opponent were trained on. High confidence, read and hash checks.** `train.py:90` samples the same registry that `evaluate.py:45` uses; `decks.py:3` explicitly includes the three fixtures among the 21 lists. All 21 current list hashes match metadata. Fresh seeds test new starts for familiar lists, not deck generalization. This is explicit in the protocols and is not a protocol defect. Held-out deck performance belongs to M3 (`docs/edison-roadmap.md:150`). Shared environment seeds also mean the three optimizers are training replications, not independent game datasets; I used the specified per-optimizer Wilson calculation rather than pretending their pooled games are independent replications.

6. **PLANTED-ONLY for this study ? privileged configuration still leaks. High confidence, source and negative control.** `ygopro.h:2567`, `:3003` and `:3028` expose hidden identities and real Deck positions with `oppo_info=True`. The privileged negative control fails on changed Deck order immediately. Study actors hardcode False (`actor.py:162`), so reaching this requires changing their configuration/path. I found no path that turns it on in study play. I did not reproduce the unrelated same-code alias harness, unsupported prompt scripts, or Morale Boost defect; there is no evidence here that any affects these lists or recorded cells.
